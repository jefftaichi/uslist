About USList.info

Where the listings come from

Every business here is drawn from OpenStreetMap, the open map of the world maintained by volunteers and businesses themselves. Names, addresses, phone numbers, and websites come straight from that data.

What it doesn't include

No reviews, ratings, or photos — that content isn't part of open map data. Coverage is strongest for storefronts like restaurants, retail, lodging, and health care, and thinner for services run without a physical location.

How it's kept current

Listings are collected ahead of time and saved as fixed files, so pages load instantly and nothing depends on a live service. We regularly refresh the directory to re-collect the data and republishes the files.

Finding places near you

On any category page you can share your location or type a ZIP code to sort results by distance. Nothing is stored or sent anywhere — the sorting happens in your own browser. ZIP code centers come from GeoNames.

Current coverage

728,727 listings across 1397 cities.

A listing looks wrong? Anyone can correct it at openstreetmap.org, and the fix flows into this directory on the next refresh.
