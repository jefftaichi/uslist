People Search Terms

Last updated: October 2026. These terms apply only to the people search.

What these tools are

The USList.info people search connects you to people-search reports sold by our partners. USList.info does not provide the people-finder data, and does not hold, sell or verify personal records. All records are owned and supplied by the partners.

Not a consumer reporting agency

Neither USList.info nor our partners are consumer reporting agencies under the Fair Credit Reporting Act (FCRA, 15 U.S.C. § 1681 et seq.). Reports are not consumer reports.

Prohibited uses

You may not use any search or report to decide eligibility for employment, tenant screening, credit, insurance, educational admission, hiring household workers, or any other purpose covered by the FCRA. You may not use results to stalk, harass, or harm anyone, or for any unlawful purpose.

Accuracy

Records come from public and third-party sources and may be incomplete, outdated or wrong. Do not rely on them as proof of identity, criminal history or ownership.

Purchases and subscriptions

Reports are paid products. Pricing, trials, billing, cancellations and refunds are set by the partner and governed by the partner's own terms and privacy policy, which you accept on their site.

Advertising disclosure

Links to partners are affiliate links. USList.info may earn a commission when you purchase. This never changes the price you pay.

Data ownership, removals and updates

USList.info does not provide the people-finder data. Every record shown through the search is supplied by Spokeo, an independent provider, and we have no ability to edit, correct, update or delete it. To have your information removed, corrected or updated, contact Spokeo directly through its opt-out page at https://www.spokeo.com/optout or its support pages. Requests sent to USList.info cannot be actioned, because the data never passes through us.

Privacy

Names you enter are passed to the partner to run your search. USList.info does not collect, store or share them. The search is for adults 18 and older and is not directed to children. See our main Privacy Policy for site analytics and advertising.

Changes

We may update these terms at any time. Continued use of the search tools means you accept the current version.
