Contact USList.info

Use the form below for general questions about the directory. It reaches us directly, and it is the only contact method we publish — we keep our email address off the site so it can’t be harvested by spammers. Every field is required.

Please note: changes to a business listing — corrections, additions, or removals — are made on OpenStreetMap, the open map project our listings come from, not here. We put together a step-by-step guide to adding or correcting a business to make that easy.

We use your email only to reply.

Changing a listing

Listing details come from OpenStreetMap. Editing the original record there is the right way to fix a name, address, phone number, or website, or to remove a business — and it improves every site that uses the data, not just ours. Our pages pick up those edits the next time we refresh.

Response time

This is a small, independently run directory, so replies usually take a few business days.

Not for sales pitches
